Picture
Open a screenshot, drop one onto this window, or press Ctrl+V to paste one.

Mark up a screenshot for a ticket, a manual or a chat: arrows, boxes, numbered steps, text, highlights, and areas that are really blacked out or pixelated. It all happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded and your original file is never touched. The saved picture carries no hidden camera or screen data.

    Pixelate and blur leave a little of the detail; for text or numbers use solid black.

    Pick a tool and drag on the picture. Use Select to move, resize or delete an item (Delete key).
