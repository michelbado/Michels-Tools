Picture
JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. You can also drop a picture onto this window.

Hide faces, number plates, names, screens or documents in a picture. It all happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded and your original file is never touched. The saved copy has the hidden parts really changed (not just covered by a layer) and carries no hidden camera data. Solid black is the only choice that can never be undone. Pixelating and blurring remove most of the detail, but for text, numbers or anything where a guess could be enough, use black.

    Drag on the picture to mark an area. Click a marked area to select it: drag it to move, drag a corner to resize, press Delete to remove it.
