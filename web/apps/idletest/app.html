How long to wait

Between you and this site there may be a proxy, a CDN, a load balancer or a firewall. Each has a timer that cuts a connection that has been quiet for too long, and that breaks long downloads, reports that take a while to build and live connections. This test asks the server to keep two requests open without sending anything (one of them with a tiny heartbeat) and shows when something cuts them. Keep this window open and the computer awake. Nothing you type or any file is used.
