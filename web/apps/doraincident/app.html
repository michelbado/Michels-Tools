How far can you trust this?

  • The result is only as good as your answers. Several lines are judgement calls, such as "likely to lose clients" or "significant impact".

  • "Does not meet the criteria" is the result to double-check: look again whenever the facts change.
  • The tool files nothing and cannot see your other incidents, your contracts or your authority's instructions.
