The link

The link MTU is the largest packet the physical link carries: 1500 on normal Ethernet, 9000 or more on a jumbo-frame network. "Traffic you carry" is the kind of packets whose size you want to know; "Tunnels run over" is the kind of packets the tunnels themselves travel in.

What the packet goes through, outermost layer first

Pick the layers in the order the packet meets them on its way out. IPsec padding changes with the packet size, so for IPsec the result is the largest packet that still fits. This is arithmetic only: nothing is read from your device or network, and nothing is sent anywhere.
