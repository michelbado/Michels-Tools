JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. You can also drop a picture onto this window.

Change the type of a picture and make the file smaller. It all happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded and your original file is never touched. The saved copy carries no hidden camera data (no location, no camera details, no colour profile). A GIF becomes a still picture.
