Picture
JPEG, PNG or WebP. You can also drop a picture onto this window.

Pictures carry hidden details: the camera, the date, the exact place, sometimes serial numbers and the owner's name. Here you can read them, change or remove them, and save a clean copy. The picture itself is not re-compressed, so its quality does not change. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded, and your original file is never touched.

    What this picture gives away
      Edit details (also works on a picture that has none: just fill in what you want)

      A box that already has a value shows what the picture contains now. Change it to replace it, or empty it to remove it. A box that is empty and stays empty adds nothing. Text can use plain letters and digits only (accents are simplified, other characters become "?"). Latitude is positive in the north and negative in the south; longitude is positive in the east and negative in the west. Typing a place here does not make the picture truthful: it only writes what you type.

      What to do

      These buttons only tick and untick the boxes below. They also clear or restore the boxes above. Then you can still change single items: untick a box in the list below to remove that item.
