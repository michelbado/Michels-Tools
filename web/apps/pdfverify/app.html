This checks that each signature is mathematically valid and that the document has not been changed since it was signed. It does not check whether the signer's certificate is trusted by a recognised authority, or whether it has been revoked. A valid signature from a certificate anyone could have made proves the file is unchanged, not who signed it.

Open a PDF, or drop one onto this window.

You will see every digital signature in it: who signed, when, what the certificate says, and whether the document was changed after signing.

The file is read inside your browser. It is not uploaded.
