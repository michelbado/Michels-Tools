The bytes of one packet, in hex

Copy the bytes of one packet from a capture (tcpdump, Wireshark, a switch's capture, a log) and paste them here. The decoder names every header field, checks the checksums it can, and points out what looks wrong. Click a row to see its bytes. Everything happens in your browser: the bytes are never sent anywhere. A capture shows only what the capture saw: encrypted data (ESP, TLS) stays unreadable.
