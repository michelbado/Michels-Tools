AI Use Case Intake & Assessment
Describe an AI use case and answer what you know. The tool brings questions from several regulations together, tells you which rules apply, what class the use falls in, what is missing, and what to do about it. Leave the rest at "Not sure": unknown is a finding, not a pass.
A decision aid, not a legal opinion. It follows a practitioner's method and summaries of the rules, which may be incomplete, out of date or wrong for your case. Check the current texts and ask a qualified lawyer.
How far can you trust this?
  • It scores nothing. Classification (a legal question) is kept apart from controls (a management question), a critical gap is never offset by good answers elsewhere, and a "Not sure" is never counted as compliant.
  • The result is only as good as your answers. "A person reviews it" and "it is just a tool" are claims to test, not facts.
  • The rules are the author's summaries of the EU AI Act, GDPR, DORA and others. Some of them may be secondary or unofficial, so check the current texts. The legal basis of a question is shown under it where there is one.
  • The tool files nothing and sends nothing: closing this window clears it. Save your answers to a file on your own computer to continue later.
