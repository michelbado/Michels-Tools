DNS text records (TXT)

Paste one or several records: SPF (starts with v=spf1), DMARC (v=DMARC1) or a DKIM key (a p= tag). Quotes, split strings and zone-file lines are fine. The tool explains each part in plain words and points out mistakes. It cannot look anything up: it works only on what you paste, nothing is sent anywhere, and SPF "include" records are not followed (so the lookup count is a lower bound). To see the records of a domain, run dig TXT example.com, dig TXT _dmarc.example.com or dig TXT selector._domainkey.example.com, or ask your DNS provider.
